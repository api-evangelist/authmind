AI agents are showing up across the enterprise faster than security teams can track them. Many now hold credentials, touch systems, and make decisions, all without a human reviewing every (or any) step. That gap is a big part of why Gartner's continued coverage of the Identity Visibility and Intelligence Platforms (IVIP) category, a space Gartner has been tracking for about a year now, is worth paying attention to now, not down the road.
