Nobody knowingly provisions a shadow AI agent. It doesn't go through change management, it doesn't get a ticket, and it doesn't show up on an asset inventory. It can spin up immediately the moment an employee installs a GenAI coding assistant, connects a personal account to a SaaS tool or lets one autonomous workflow kick off another.
